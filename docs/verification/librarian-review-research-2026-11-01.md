# Research behind Librarian Development and Vault Review

Consulted 2026-11-01. These sources inform guidance, not mandatory rules for an author's fiction. No source is copied as a scripted interview, and runtime conversations do not browse or transmit vault contents to these sites.

- [Brandon Sanderson, Sanderson’s Second Law](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/sandersons-second-law): examines how limitations and costs can matter to a fictional power's dramatic usefulness. Application: ask about constraints, access, tradeoffs and consequences when those matter to the actual story; compare declared rules with use in scenes.
- [Patricia C. Wrede, Magic Worldbuilding](https://pcwrede.com/pcw-wp/magic-worldbuilding/): distinguishes the author's process from what the story must explain, and discusses limits/costs without demanding a scientific rulebook. Application: respect soft systems, mystery, atmosphere and author preference; do not flag every unstated mechanism as a plot hole.
- [Reza et al., Co-Writing with AI, on Human Terms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12488): a systematic review of 109 HCI papers, complemented by writer interviews, discussing guided exploration, critical feedback and author agency across writing activities. Application (our design inference): keep exploratory conversation distinct from canon, retain author decisions and require individual acceptance of edits.
- [Rashkin et al., Help Me Write a Story](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16007): evaluates writing feedback on deliberately corrupted stories; reports useful feedback alongside failures to identify the biggest issue or calibrate praise/criticism. Application (our design inference): separate supported contradictions from tentative concerns, verify quoted evidence, prioritize actions and disclose review coverage without claiming completeness.

The implementation's separation of deterministic timeline checks and AI thinking comes from the owner's explicit direction, not from treating literary advice as a mechanical validator. Questions are generated from the entry type, original material and ongoing conversation; no fixed question list is supplied.

Transport reliability also follows [Ollama's structured-output interface](https://ollama.com/blog/structured-outputs): review requests explicitly select JSON output, while ordinary Ask/writing requests retain their existing format. A tested compatibility repair escapes literal control characters inside JSON strings returned by some local servers; it does not guess missing structure or fields. The native pilot exposed this concrete case before completion.
